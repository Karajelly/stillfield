## What this means for forks

You are welcome to fork, modify, and redistribute the code under the MIT License. If you publish your own build, please replace the reserved artwork with your own and use a different name, icon, domain, and bundle identifier, so that it is not mistaken for the original Stillfield.

You may keep the unmodified artwork in a fork or clone for local development and for making contributions back to this repository.

To ask about other uses of the artwork, open an issue.
