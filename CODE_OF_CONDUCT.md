# Code of conduct

Stillfield is a calm project, and its community should be too.

## Our standard

Be kind, patient, and respectful. Assume good intent, give feedback on the work and not the person, and welcome people of every background and experience level.

Not acceptable: harassment, personal attacks, insults, discrimination, sexualized language or attention, doxxing, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and comments, as well as anywhere you represent the project publicly.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting: open a [private advisory](https://github.com/Karajelly/stillfield/security/advisories/new) and mark it as a conduct issue in the title, or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who violate this code, at their discretion.
